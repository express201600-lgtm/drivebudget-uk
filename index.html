UK driving guides

How much should you budget for driving lessons?

Multiply your instructor's hourly rate by the hours you expect to need. Keep extra room in your budget for tests, extra lessons and any instructor car hire for the practical test. Compare actual local instructor quotes rather than relying on one UK-wide estimate.

Official driving test fees (GOV.UK)

Manual or automatic: which is right for you?

Automatic lessons may simplify gear changes, but hourly rates and total learning time vary. A manual licence generally lets you drive both types, whereas an automatic-only licence limits you to automatic vehicles. Compare instructor availability, lesson costs and the cars you want to own.

Upgrading an automatic licence (GOV.UK)

What does your first car really cost?

Include the purchase price or finance, insurance, fuel, vehicle tax, maintenance, MOT and parking. New drivers should obtain real insurance quotes before choosing a car. Budget for repairs even if the car is inexpensive.

Vehicle tax information (GOV.UK)

How to compare car finance offers

Compare the total amount payable, not just the monthly figure. Check APR, fees, deposit, contract length and whether a final balloon payment is due. Consider affordability if your circumstances change.
